# TideWall — Story

TideWall tells two stories at once. The **surface story** is told in words: title cards, banners, a few lines from the
people of the town. The **hidden story** is never written down anywhere in the game. It is told only in symbols:
marks on stone, on shells, on sails, on the money in your pocket. A player who only reads will finish a war story. A
player who looks will find out why the war never ends, and that they can end it.

This file is the key. It spoils everything.

---

## The surface story

**Saltmarrow** is a fishing town on a long beach, behind a sea wall, around the **Nuclear Sand Powerplant**: a glowing
green core that lights the town, warms the hotdog stand, and (everyone agrees) is the only reason Saltmarrow is still
here. Every night the sea sends creatures up the sand to break the wall and reach the core. Every morning the town sells
the bodies, patches the wall, and pays its defenders in sand dollars.

**Part I: The Wall.** Hold the wall. Keep the core alive. Survive as many days as you can.

**Part II: The Undertow.** The town's engineers find a way to turn the war around: run the core past its limit and the
sea *pulls back*. For one long night the water stands up in two walls along the bay and the seabed lies open, all the
way down to where the tides come from. The sailors march out to end the war at its source: the **Heart of the Tide**.

| Stage | Name | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Ebb** | Cross the drained shallows. Topple the brood spires the creatures hatch from. |
| 2 | **The Kelp Cathedral** | Light the old beacons in a forest of kelp and stone arches, and hold them while the sea's things come for the light. |
| 3 | **The Hull Graveyard** | A field of wrecked ships blocks the way down. Carry fire (gas cans) to the wreck-wall and blow a way through. |
| 4 | **The Drowned Keep** | A ruined keep on the sea floor. Hold it while the deep sends everything it has. |
| 5 | **The Heart** | The trench. The **Tidemother** guards the Heart of the Tide. Beat her. Then decide. |

The **Low Tide Engine** (the overdriven core) can only hold the sea back so long: every stage has a tide clock. If it
runs out, the water comes home, and the expedition with it. (Part I ends when the core melts down; Part II ends when the
core gives out. Same core, opposite failure.)

At the Heart the obvious choice is **Strike the Heart**. The game says: the sea falls quiet; Saltmarrow is safe. It
fades to the town, the wall, the morning. *Part I begins again.*

## The hidden story (never written in the game)

**Long ago the sea and five people made a pact.** The sea gave them a light (a heart, a pearl that holds the tide) so
the five could live on the shore. They gave it their word to return it. They didn't. They built a keep around the
light, then a town, and when the sea came to take its heart back, a wall. The light became a "powerplant". The war is
the sea asking for its heart, every night, forever. The heart is also the only reason the town can push the sea back
at all: the Low Tide Engine works *because* the core is the sea's own heart, and the tide obeys it.

Every few generations Saltmarrow marches out at low tide, kills the sea's guardian and smashes the new heart she has
grown, and the war starts over. The town calls this winning. The sea floor is covered with the wrecks of every
expedition that "won".

The way out is to give the heart back.

### The symbols

| Symbol | Looks like | Means | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Spiral** | a full spiral | the sea; the tide; the cycle | creatures' shells (inner half), the Tidemother, the Heart, the drowned keep, the water walls of Part II, the peace ending |
| **The Crest** | a ring broken open, with a tail | Saltmarrow's town crest. It is *the outer half of the spiral*: the part that was taken | the gatehouse banners, the keep, the powerplant, every Saltmarrow sail, every wreck |
| **The Mark** | a small inner curl | *the inner half of the spiral*: what the sea kept | on Clawbacks, crocs, surfboards, the Tidemother. Put the Crest and the Mark together and they make the Spiral |
| **Five petals** | the sand dollar's five-petal flower | the five who made the pact. The town's money is stamped with it, and is literally the skeletons of sea creatures: Saltmarrow pays its soldiers in the sea's bones | the money icon on every HUD, the five seats around the Heart's hollow, the five Spiral Shards, the five stages |
| **Tally marks** | strokes in fives | how many times. Every expedition. Every run | the keep's wall in the main menu (one mark per run you've played), the wrecks in the Hull Graveyard (one wreck per run), scratches on the Tidemother's shell |
| **The Hand** | an open hand | a promise / taking | pact murals: two hands joined (the pact), one hand closing on the heart (the theft) |
| **The Eye** | an eye | the sea watching, remembering | on the brood spires, over the drowned keep's door |
| **The Heart** | a circle with rays | the light / the heart | the powerplant's core (look closely at it in Part I: the Spiral is in there), the Heart in the trench, the empty hollow next to it that is exactly the core's shape |

### How the symbols tell it, stage by stage

- **Part I**: The crest is on the gatehouse banners, the keep, the powerplant. The creatures carry the Mark: a curl that
  fits *inside* the crest. The core's glow has a spiral in it. Gulls come at dawn and carry the dead out to sea
  (they're not thieves; they're taking their dead home). Your money is five petals. The keep wall you see from the main
  menu gets a tally mark for every run you play.
- **The Ebb**: beached Saltmarrow boats, crest on the sails. The brood spires are carved with the Spiral and an Eye.
- **The Kelp Cathedral**: stone arches older than the town. Their murals: the Spiral and five figures; two hands joined;
  a heart passed from the sea to the five; a calm wave. *The pact.* The beacons the sailors light are the sea's own
  lamps.
- **The Hull Graveyard**: the wreck-wall is made of Saltmarrow ships, every one with the crest and tally marks. There is
  one wreck for each run the player has played (the same count as the keep wall). You are not the first expedition. You
  are the latest.
- **The Drowned Keep**: the same keep as the town's, stone for stone, on the sea floor: the five's first home, taken by
  the sea when they broke the pact. Its murals: five figures carrying the heart away; a keep; the wave rising; a wall.
  *The theft.* The Eye is over the door.
- **The Heart**: the Tidemother carries the Mark on her shell and a tally of scratches. Beside the new Heart she guards
  is an empty hollow the exact shape of Saltmarrow's core, ringed by five petal-seats. *The first heart was hers.*

### The Spiral Shards and the second ending

One **Spiral Shard** is hidden in each stage, off the main path: a stone with one petal and one arc of the Crest. They
are kept between runs. With all five, when the Tidemother falls, a second choice appears at the Heart beside **Strike
the Heart**: **Lay down the Crest**. The five shards close into the Crest, the Tidemother's Mark completes it, and the
Spiral is whole for the first time. The ending: the core in Saltmarrow goes dark, the water walls fall gently, and the
main menu changes for good. The sea outside the keep window is calm, there are no more creatures on the beach, the
wall's banners show the full Spiral, and the tally on the keep wall stops.

Nobody in the game ever says what happened. The symbols do.

## Writing rules (for adding to the story)

- **Never explain the hidden story in text.** Not in banners, cards, item descriptions or vendor lines. Titles and
  epigraphs may be ambiguous; they may not be explanatory.
- **Every symbol means one thing**, and is used the same way everywhere (see the table). New symbols need a row in the
  table first.
- **The surface story stays sincere.** The town believes it is the hero. The sailors are brave. The game never mocks
  them for not seeing it.
- **Counting matters.** Five founders, five stages, five petals, five shards, tally marks in fives. Keep new content in
  fives where it can be.
- New parts go after Part II in `Campaign.asset` (see `docs/TECH.md` → Campaign). Each part should add a layer to the
  hidden story, not reveal it outright.
